# Community and contributor conduct

Participate respectfully. No harassment, discrimination, doxxing, impersonation, scams, coordinated manipulation, undisclosed promotion, or fabricated verification claims. Disagree with a trade thesis using evidence and discuss risks without attacking its author.

Members must disclose relevant positions and compensation as required by the publishing workflow. Losses, unresolved trades, and criticism cannot be removed merely to improve a reputation score. Moderation may hide harmful content, restrict accounts, and review appeals with recorded reasons.

Report community content using its in-product report action when available. Report contributor conduct privately to the project owner. The owner must publish monitored contacts and moderation hours before beta. Appeals are reviewed by a moderator other than the original decision maker where staffing permits.

These rules apply to repository contributions, product discussion, and official community spaces. Implementation detail is in [community requirements](docs/product/requirements.md).
