# Marketing content and policy implementation

Working brand: Trading Community. Replace through one configuration source when the founder supplies the final name/domain. This is complete implementation copy for product facts, not signed legal policy text.

## Core copy

Home headline: “Trade ideas with a history you can inspect.”

Supporting text: “Publish stock, options, and futures plans with annotated charts. Discuss the reasoning, record reported outcomes, and build a trading journal.”

Primary CTA: “Create free account”. Secondary: “Explore ideas”.

Three workflow cards:
- Plan and annotate: Choose a supported instrument, explain your thesis, and mark entries, targets, and invalidation.
- Discuss the setup: Ask questions, compare reasoning, and follow authors and instruments.
- Report and review: Record your own execution reports, inspect visible updates, and learn from complete outcomes.

Evidence note: “Execution prices and results are reported by members. They are not verified against brokerage accounts.”

Data note: “Market data is delayed. Source timestamps and delay labels appear alongside supported data.”

Pro text: “More room for your research workflow: expanded watchlists and saved layouts, deeper journal filters, and analytical exports.” Public credibility is not a paid benefit.

Do not advertise availability until release gates pass. In preview/development, use “Product preview” and visibly synthetic examples. Do not invent testimonials, awards, partner logos, users, profit screenshots, urgency countdowns, or live statistics.

## Pricing and FAQ

Display the complete [MVP plan table](../mvp-specification.md), not a shortened sales table that hides limits. Annual price is $290/year; do not label a rounded monthly equivalent as a monthly charge. Taxes are additional where applicable. No free trial, coupon, lifetime plan, team plan, or creator subscription in this baseline.

FAQ answers:
- Can I place orders? Members execute trades separately with their brokers; the platform records their reports.
- Are results verified? No. Publications show reporting timestamps, exclusions, cost coverage, and unresolved records.
- Is data real time? Both plans use licensed delayed data with visible timestamps.
- Which options are supported? Long calls/puts and the four defined same-expiration verticals; explain unsupported adjustments/assignment handling.
- Which futures? Listed ES, MES, NQ, and MNQ contracts.
- What happens after cancellation? Paid benefits continue through the paid period; records remain accessible/exportable after downgrade.
- Does Pro increase my visibility or win-rate credibility? No.
- Can I livestream or use scanners? Those capabilities are deferred.
- Can I keep notes private? Yes, journal notes and drafts are owner-only; published chart notes are public.
- How do I report a problem? Use report, appeal, or support; show actual coverage/contact details once staffed.

## Policy routes

Implement versioned content pages and acceptance recording for Terms, Privacy, Risk Disclosure, Community Guidelines, and relevant purchase terms. A content configuration field tracks draft/reviewed/approved status, policy version, effective date, approver reference, and publication date. Draft policy content cannot be presented as approved in production.

Counsel/operator review must resolve:
- Legal operator name/address/contact, applicable jurisdictions, adult US participation declarations.
- Member-content license necessary for hosting/share images/reposts, IP complaints, moderation, appeals, prohibited conduct.
- Actual product classification/disclosures; no assumed publisher/adviser exemption.
- Subscription renewal/cancel/refund/tax treatment and processor requirements.
- Actual collection/purpose/processors/retention, deletion/export/support access and regional rights.
- Supplier-specific display, attribution, retention, screenshots, derived data, and export restrictions.
- Support/incident contacts, record holds, and security incident communications.

These are external approval inputs with named gates, not missing engineering behavior. Build page layout, versioning, consent storage, export/delete mechanics, and draft protections using fixtures before copy approval. Do not fabricate legal entity/contact placeholders in a public release.

## SEO and content delivery

Public pages have unique title/description, canonical URL, social metadata, accessible headings, and appropriate structured data only for actual page facts. Publication metadata uses the public title/author and licensed image or safe text-only fallback. Private/admin/auth/checkout-result/preview routes are noindex and excluded from public sitemaps.
