# ADR 0019: Frozen publication jobs and reported lifecycle

Date: October 20, 2026. Status: engineering implementation for P08; local evidence and remaining browser/release gates are recorded in [P08](../implementation/evidence/P08.md).

## Decision

The composer saves private incomplete drafts, then reviews a specific draft/chart revision. `POST /drafts/{id}/publish` requires matching strong revision, CSRF, idempotency key, a complete supported plan, current independently checked provider/database rights, and up to three owned processed attachments. A shared ten-post UTC daily limit applies to both plans. It freezes an immutable candidate and returns a private 202 job. A public timestamp, current version and position exist publicly only after trusted image processing commits successfully. A failure preserves the candidate and recoverable draft; arbitrary uploaded chart screenshots cannot satisfy publication.

The durable outbox dispatches a bounded worker browser job. The pinned Playwright 1.64.0 worker image contains its matching browser; Node and pnpm retain the agreed versions. No host browser installation is introduced. A signed token binds job, version, one-time nonce and short expiry. The internal render route has no ambient session, claims that candidate once, and renders frozen bars, semantic drawings and source/reporting notices. Network access is restricted to configured application resources. The worker validates a 1200×760 PNG, re-encodes it to WebP, writes immutable bytes, and submits the exact object/checksum binding. The API alone advances visibility. The worker waits for an explicit ready/error marker, reports bounded stage codes and reconciles expired/lost or exhausted jobs without changing frozen candidates. Stable outbox UUIDs deduplicate queue delivery; terminal failures release initial draft locks or return corrections to review. The product worker is not a substitute for the agent's blocked interactive UI acceptance.

Stocks support long/short; standard options support long call, long put and the four agreed verticals; futures use actual dated ES/MES/NQ/MNQ contracts. Options retain an underlying chart and separate premium/spread values. Educational publications have no position. Actual contract metadata is hydrated on the server, not accepted from a browser multiplier/strike claim. The composer estimate uses the same decimal-safe domain, with server revalidation at commit.

One reported position per publication freezes its initial planned risk and source version. Member fill prices drive realized cash calculations; they do not move that denominator. An actual fill can cross an originally planned stop without invalidating the historical plan. One grouped entry, at most three proportional partial exits, and a full remaining final exit are enforced. Every report retains claimed UTC and server receipt separately. Original publication, frozen entry window and five-minute receipt determine forward eligibility; late reports remain visible as retrospective. Missing fees or short-stock borrowing costs remain unknown and exclude incomplete results from complete-cost headlines.

New claimed entries at or after expiry are rejected; a delayed receipt for a pre-expiry claim remains reportable. Expiry/assignment/settlement exceptions require explicit resolution and review. A second active resolution must use the visible correction flow, and resolution/review chronology cannot precede the existing lifecycle. `netResult` on a resolution is the net cash result for unresolved remaining units, including their attributable acquisition and settlement costs; previously realized partial exits are added once. R is recomputed by exact rational division against frozen risk. No synthetic fill, target hit or default zero settlement is invented.

Text corrections append a visible version with a reason. Structured corrections require independent staff review and recent MFA. A changed chart/plan keeps the current evidence visible while a new trusted snapshot is processed. Corrected reports append superseding events and a new calculation snapshot; originals remain in public history. Public version/history reads expose committed visible lineage, never a private pending or failed replacement. Text corrections allocate sequence numbers above all reserved candidates, including failed ones. Event-only corrections can reuse the nearest committed ancestor’s frozen source after a text edit, with current text/plan overlaid; they remain possible when new option publishing is held but current public retention rights permit historical evidence. Risk and outcome provenance identify their respective source versions. Current market context is requested separately and cannot rewrite original evidence. Privacy/deletion policy retains precedence over permanence claims.

Public reported performance uses eligible, complete-cost closed outcomes and discloses wins/losses/breakevens, denominator, cost coverage, retrospective/open/unresolved exclusions, reported cash P&L and mean R. It is not a portfolio/account return, broker verification, or cross-asset dollar ranking. Editorial eligibility describes completeness, transparency and follow-up for manual review; payment and win rate do not purchase placement. P09 community actions and P13 editorial selection remain separate milestones.

## Consequences

Three additive immutable migrations introduce publication jobs, attachment bindings, event details, correction review/job references and risk/outcome version provenance (53 Prisma models, thirteen migrations). Canonical operations remain 125; additive inline fields preserve existing client compatibility. Position event writes now explicitly require `If-Match`, with idempotent replay resolved before stale revision rejection. API, web and worker share a server-only render HMAC secret. Worker capture increases local image size and needs shared memory; browser/channel updates must stay synchronized with the exact package and image digest.

Synthetic local fixture acceptance is distinct from commercial supplier conformance. Browser/touch, independent device, screen-reader and visual approval evidence remain open where the supported browser tool is blocked. No deployment, purchase, remote CI result or legal approval follows from this ADR.
