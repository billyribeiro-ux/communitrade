# ADR 0024: Assigned moderation, scoped support and recoverable privacy erasure

Date: October 20, 2026. Status: engineering implementation. Actual acceptance and open release gates are recorded in [P13 evidence](../implementation/evidence/P13.md). Implements REQ-010, REQ-022, REQ-023, REQ-027 and REQ-032; supplements the existing identity, publication, notification and billing decisions.

## Decision

Staff permissions are exact action/resource allowlists. Moderators see their assigned report/appeal cases and corresponding audits; administrators manage assignment, accounts, editorial selections, support, privacy, data, billing and jobs. Current grants are checked on the server. Sensitive administrator reads and commands require recent server-verified MFA. Mutations require CSRF, stable command identity, a reason and immutable audit. A reporter, target author or original reviewer cannot decide an appeal; an independent assigned reviewer chooses uphold or overturn. Authors retain no ability to erase criticism. Suspension and role changes revoke sessions. Last-active-administrator protection covers suspension and demotion to member or moderator, including competing requests.

Publication moderation uses `moderation_hidden`, distinct from owner hiding and privacy removal. Restoration only undoes the moderation state. Profile/repost source markers also preserve reversible state. Public reads, author projections, search, discovery, reactions, notifications and derivative access recheck source visibility rather than trusting cached links. Existing reviewed correction capture/version/calculation controls remain authoritative; staff cannot write performance scores.

Support intake gives a private reference and queues a generic receipt without revealing account existence or echoing submitted content into email. Authenticated intake binds the verified account email. Anonymous tickets require an explicit verified-email claim; knowing a reference alone grants no access. Replies, assignment and resolution persist with owner/staff authorization. Contact mailbox and staffing readiness are deployment configuration, with a production startup guard; a boolean flag is not an approval artifact.

Ordinary staff have no journal browser. An owner names one owned journal entry or draft and a purpose inside a ticket. A different administrator with recent MFA may approve access for that named administrator for at most one hour. Every read rechecks the principal, role, owner/account state, resource, purpose, expiry and grant status and writes an audit without private content. Owners and administrators can revoke grants; ticket resolution revokes approved access. No global escalation role exists.

Full personal archives reuse the durable export queue, private storage and owner-authenticated 24-hour download. They contain account/community/workspace/support/billing data but exclude credentials and other members' private data. Collection rows and aggregate bytes have explicit limits; overflow fails rather than silently truncating. JSON archive keys are separate from formula-safe CSV exports. A repeatable-read snapshot and final owner/account check fence publication of the artifact.

Deletion requires verified recent identity and an explicit confirmation. Requested deletions have a 30-day review deadline; an administrator can queue earlier processing or place a reasoned, finite hold. Requests reaching their deadline are automatically eligible; held requests release at their recorded expiry or through an explicit staff release. Billing cancellation remains an explicit processor action under P12 and is disclosed separately from deletion. Retained financial/audit/moderation records have a documented operational purpose and retention review, not unlimited personal-content retention.

Erasure uses durable stages, leases, object inventory, safe failure codes and idempotent retries. It revokes access before deleting objects/private records and redacting public personal content. Historical versions keep chronology and a bounded financial projection, while authored prose, title, drawings/annotations and unknown nested payloads are redacted. Normal publication/chart/position-event immutability remains enforced. Narrow database trigger exceptions require the correct in-progress privacy request, owning resource, stage and exact permitted redaction shape; they do not permit arbitrary trade edits. Applied migrations remain immutable; later guard refinements are additive.

The minimal deletion suppression ledger stores a keyed digest of issuer/subject, key version and request reference. Authentication checks it before inserting or restoring an identity. Restore tooling imports the ledger before traffic resumes, revokes matching restored accounts and queues normal erasure idempotently. The HMAC key and newer ledger must be backed up independently of an older application snapshot; losing either blocks a compliant restore. Local mode can reuse the existing session secret; production requires a stable explicit privacy key.

## Consequences and limits

This implements local operational workflows rather than decorative queues. Synthetic SMTP/processor and fixture data do not prove monitored staffing, counsel-approved retention/hold policy, production deliverability, real processor conformance or production recovery objectives. A database erasure cannot recall already downloaded copies or third-party caches. Provider-side identity/processor retention and backups require their separate operational policies and evidence. No Phase 2 rooms, streaming, scanners, broker integration or payouts are added.
