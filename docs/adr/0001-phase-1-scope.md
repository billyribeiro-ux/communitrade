# ADR 0001 Complete Phase 1 product scope

Date: October 19, 2026
Status: Accepted product direction

## Context

The founder confirmed the entire business platform: public marketing, accounts, subscriptions, public trade ideas/community, member workspace and administration. Stocks, six supported option structures, and four futures roots launch together.

## Decision

Use the MVP and requirement registry as the complete release baseline. Close profile/share/marketing details through companion specs. Keep streaming/live chat in Phase 2 and scanners/broker/creator features deferred.

## Consequences

A sketch is not scope. Every surface has routes, acceptance and a milestone. Market-data, legal and processor gates remain external evidence.

## Alternatives and revisit trigger

Use [system architecture](../architecture/system-design.md), [integration contracts](../architecture/integrations.md), and [requirements](../product/requirements.md) for the detailed boundary. Revisit if supported versions, measured performance, legal/supplier terms, founder scope or release evidence invalidate the decision. Preserve the original decision and document the replacement rather than silently changing the stack.
