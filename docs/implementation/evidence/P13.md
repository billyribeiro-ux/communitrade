Date: October 20, 2026. Requirements REQ-010, REQ-022, REQ-023, REQ-027 and REQ-032; acceptance AC-010, AC-022, AC-023, AC-027 and AC-032. Local implementation and automated acceptance are complete. Rendered visual/keyboard acceptance and external operational approvals remain open. No deployment, paid service, Git commit/PR, commercial approval or production recovery claim is made.

## Delivered scope

Assigned moderation and independent appeals; account roles/suspension/session revocation and last-admin protection; reasoned editorial selection; role-scoped audit search/export and staff overview; retained corrections/instrument/data/billing/job controls. Full private support intake/claim/replies/receipts/assignment and expiring, named, owner-requested resource access. Personal JSON archives, verified deletion requests, finite holds, staged erasure, immutable-history privacy redaction, keyed suppression and restore reapplication. All authored UI uses CSS/CSS Modules and Phosphor. See [ADR 0024](../../adr/0024-staff-support-privacy-control.md).

- Reports and appeals persist assignment, decisions, reasons and notices. Moderators see assigned cases and corresponding audits; administrators manage assignment and the wider operational queues. Reporters, affected authors and the original reviewer cannot decide their own appeal. Member case projections withhold reporter-private detail and private appeal prose from the opposing party. Publication `moderation_hidden` is distinct from owner hiding and privacy removal; restoration cannot undo those other states.
- Current role/account state, recent server-verified administrator MFA, CSRF, exact action allowlists, stable command identity and immutable audit protect staff operations. Suspension/role changes revoke sessions. An isolated empty-role fixture proves sole-admin protection, including admin-to-moderator demotion and concurrent changes. Editorial staff select content with reasons; they cannot write performance scores. Audit CSV is bounded, authenticated and excludes private payload metadata.
- Source visibility reaches public profiles, publications, community/discovery, derivatives, attached media and performance. Direct known-ID avatar metadata/bytes and position/event reads fail for other viewers while a profile is hidden; its owner retains ownership access. Restoring the profile reopens the public projection. Existing publication-version, correction and data-rights checks remain enforced on public reads.
- Contact intake retains exact-origin validation, idempotent receipt replay and three requests per address per UTC hour. Signed-in requests require CSRF and the verified account email. Anonymous tickets need an explicit verified-email claim; a GET never claims a ticket. Private conversations, staff replies, assignment/resolution and generic durable email receipts persist. Owners can request and revoke access to one owned journal entry or draft. A recent-MFA administrator approves a named administrator for 1–60 minutes; expiry, owner/resource/account state, role and revocation are rechecked and audited on every read. There is no general journal browser. Receipt jobs appear in the safe email job channel.
- Full personal JSON archives use the existing private export worker and owner-authenticated download, expire within 24 hours, exclude credentials/other members' private records and fail explicitly on collection/byte overflow. Deletion requires an explicit confirmation and server-verified recent identity. Requests have a 30-day review deadline; administrators can process earlier or record finite holds. The worker releases expired holds and resumes recoverable staged erasure, revoking access before object/private-data deletion and controlled personal-content redaction. Immutable trade chronology and bounded financial/case/audit evidence retain their documented purpose; ordinary history remains protected.
- A separate keyed issuer/subject suppression ledger blocks new login and restored old sessions. Maintenance-only tooling exports a fresh mode-0600 ledger, imports it before traffic resumes, revokes matching restored access and queues ordinary erasure idempotently. Bounded recovery fails closed above its tested population limits. The stable HMAC key and newest ledger require separate backup custody. Billing cancellation remains an explicit P12 processor action and the privacy screen explains it.

Initial failed runs are not represented as final passes.

Repeated connected contact tests exhausted the real shared development proxy's hourly bucket. Other API/SSR checks still traverse the web proxy. PostgreSQL intake tests separately prove the limit, replay and concurrent enforcement. This is a test-client isolation change, not a production limit exception.

HTTP/SSR and product-owned trusted chart capture do not establish rendered visual, keyboard/focus, screen-reader or responsive interactive acceptance. Those G08/G12 checks remain open.
